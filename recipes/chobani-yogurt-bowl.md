The two-minute bowl for early starts and after-work hunger. Thick, tangy yogurt, something crunchy, something sweet. Honey before a run for quick fuel; berries when there's a little more time.

## Ingredients

### The bowl
- ¾ cup Chobani plain low-fat yogurt
- ⅓ cup granola or oats

### To finish
- A drizzle of honey, or
- A handful of berries

## Steps

1. Scoop the yogurt into a bowl.
2. Top with the granola or oats.
3. Finish with honey or berries (or both).
