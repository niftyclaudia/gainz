A two-potato bowl built for a long-run day. Sweet and golden potatoes roast at the start of the week; on the night, the turkey browns with peppers and cumin in one pan, the spinach wilts in the drippings, and it all goes over the potatoes. Plenty of carbs to refill and a solid dose of protein to rebuild.

## Ingredients

### The potatoes
- 1 large sweet potato
- 2 golden potatoes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt

### The pan
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 bell pepper, chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 big handfuls spinach
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt & pepper

## Steps

### Roasting the potatoes
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Cube the sweet and golden potatoes.
2. Rinse the cubes and dry them well. Dry potatoes crisp; wet ones steam.
3. Toss in olive oil and salt. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan.
4. Roast 30–35 minutes, flipping halfway, until browned at the edges.

### Building the bowl
5. Warm the oil in a skillet. Brown the turkey with the peppers, cumin and salt, breaking it up as it cooks.
6. Reheat the roasted potatoes (oven, air fryer or skillet).
7. Add the spinach to the pan and stir until just wilted. Season with pepper.
8. Pile the potatoes into bowls and spoon the turkey and spinach over the top.
