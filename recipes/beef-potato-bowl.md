The turkey bowl's heartier cousin. Crumbled beef browned hard with garlic, spinach folded in at the last minute, all of it over a pile of roasted sweet and golden potatoes. Simple, salty and filling after a ride.

## Ingredients

### The potatoes
- 1 large sweet potato
- 2 golden potatoes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt

### The pan
- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 big handfuls spinach
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt & pepper

## Steps

### Roasting the potatoes
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Cube the sweet and golden potatoes.
2. Rinse the cubes and dry them well. Dry potatoes crisp; wet ones steam.
3. Toss in olive oil and salt. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan.
4. Roast 30–35 minutes, flipping halfway, until browned at the edges.

### Building the bowl
5. Warm the oil in a skillet. Crumble in the beef and brown it with the garlic and salt.
6. Reheat the roasted potatoes (oven, air fryer or skillet).
7. Add the spinach to the pan and stir until just wilted. Season with pepper.
8. Pile the potatoes into bowls and spoon the beef and spinach over the top.
