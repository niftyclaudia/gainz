A leftovers dinner that tastes like a plan. Yesterday's pasta, some cooked chicken and two slices of turkey bacon, warmed together in one pan until the edges crisp. Carbs for tomorrow's miles, done in ten minutes.

## Ingredients

### The pan
- 1½ cups leftover cooked pasta
- 4 oz cooked chicken, sliced or shredded
- 2 slices turkey bacon

## Steps

1. Cook the turkey bacon in a skillet until crisp, then chop it.
2. Add the pasta and chicken to the same pan with a splash of water. Toss until heated through.
3. Plate and scatter the turkey bacon over the top.
